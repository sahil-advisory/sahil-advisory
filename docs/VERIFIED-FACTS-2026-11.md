## Rates for tax year 2026-27 (Finance Act 2026), all PRIMARY

UNCHANGED from FY 2025-26:
- New regime (s.202, default): 0-4L nil, 4-8L 5%, 8-12L 10%, 12-16L 15%,
  16-20L 20%, 20-24L 25%, above 24L 30%. Rebate (s.156) ₹60,000 up to ₹12 lakh
  with marginal relief. Standard deduction ₹75,000 (old regime ₹50,000).
  Family pension deduction ₹25,000 (old ₹15,000). Employer NPS 14% (new).
- Old regime still available by opting out: 0-2.5L nil, 2.5-5L 5%, 5-10L 20%,
  above 10L 30%; 60-79 nil to 3L; 80+ nil to 5L. Rebate ₹12,500 up to ₹5 lakh.
- Surcharge 10/15/25/37% (new regime max 25%; 15% cap on capital gains and
  dividends); cess 4%.
- Capital gains: STCG listed equity 20% (s.196), LTCG listed equity 12.5%
  above ₹1.25 lakh (s.198), other LTCG 12.5% without indexation (s.197).
  Holding 12 months listed, 24 months others.
- Presumptive: 44AD (s.58) ₹2 crore, ₹3 crore if cash receipts <= 5%; 6%/8%.
  44ADA ₹50 lakh, ₹75 lakh if cash <= 5%, 50%. Tax audit (s.63) ₹1 crore,
  ₹10 crore if cash receipts and payments each <= 5%; profession ₹50 lakh.

CHANGED:
- STT from 1 April 2026: futures 0.02% -> 0.05%; options premium (sale)
  0.1% -> 0.15%; exercised options 0.125% -> 0.15%.
- Buyback: from tax year 2026-27 proceeds taxed as capital gains for the
  shareholder, not dividend; promoters pay additional tax (effective 22%
  corporate, 30% others).
- TCS from 1 April 2026: LRS education/medical above ₹10 lakh 5% -> 2%; other
  LRS above ₹10 lakh 20% (unchanged); overseas tour package flat 2%, no
  threshold; liquor, scrap, coal/lignite/iron ore 1% -> 2%; tendu 5% -> 2%.
- TDS: supply of manpower is "work" (contractor rates 1%/2%); MACT interest
  exempt; automated lower/nil certificates; from 1 Oct 2026 an individual/HUF
  buying property from a non-resident needs no TAN.

TDS thresholds in force (Budget 2025, carried into s.393): bank interest
₹1 lakh senior citizens / ₹50,000 others; rent ₹50,000 per month; professional
