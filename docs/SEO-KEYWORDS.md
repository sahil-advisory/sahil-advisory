## What a number one ranking actually takes

Nobody can promise a position, and anyone who does is selling something. What
the ranking depends on, and where this site stands:

- **A page for each query, better than the incumbents.** Done for services,
  calculators, due dates and the first 13 guides. The table above is the
  backlog. ClearTax has thousands of guides; the way past that is depth on a
  narrower set, not breadth.
- **Current-year specifics.** Every calculator and guide carries FY 2025-26
  figures and a reviewed-by line. Keep them current on 1 February when the
  Budget lands; a stale slab table loses to a fresh one within weeks.
- **Links from other sites.** The site has none yet. Google Business Profile,
  ICMAI directory listing, local business directories, and the MarketsEasy
  cross-links from the blueprint are the first ten. Guest posts and press
  mentions come later.
- **Time.** A new domain takes three to six months to earn trust regardless of
  content. Traffic in month one is measured in dozens.
- **Signals Google measures directly.** Search Console will show impressions
  before clicks. Rewrite titles where impressions grow but click-through stays
  under 3%. That single loop is what moved MarketsEasy.
